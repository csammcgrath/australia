Your outbound leaves Phoenix Thu Oct 1, 4:00 pm but lands in Sydney Sat Oct 3, 6:50 am (via LAX) — the international date line eats a day. So the plan below starts Oct 3, and treats that first morning as a gentle, jet-lagged arrival rather than a sightseeing day. 01

Sydney

Ease into the trip on the harbour. No car here — the city runs on ferries and your own two feet, and parking is a tax on the foolish. Base yourself in The Rocks or Circular Quay.

Harbour city · 3 nights · no car
Oct 3
Saturday
Harbour Rocks

Land, then let the harbour wake you up

You're off a brutal flight, so keep it loose. Drop bags (rooms rarely open before mid-afternoon — arrange left-luggage), then do the one walk that fixes jet lag: the harbour foreshore.

  • Royal Botanic Garden through to Mrs Macquarie's Point for the postcard Opera House + Bridge line-up
  • Loop back through Circular Quay to The Rocks for an early, easy dinner
Fight the long afternoon nap. Push to a normal bedtime and the rest of the trip's body-clock sorts itself out.
Oct 4
Sunday
Harbour Rocks

The Manly ferry, then a slow city afternoon

Pick one big outing, not two — cramming Manly and the Bondi-to-Coogee walk into one day turns a holiday into a forced march. The ferry ride itself is half the reason to go north.

  • Morning: ferry from Circular Quay to Manly, walk to Shelly Beach, lunch by the water
  • Afternoon: back across the harbour, wander Barangaroo or Surry Hills at your own pace
Saving Bondi–Coogee? It keeps. Swap it in for Manly if you'd rather have the cliff walk — but don't try to do both.
Oct 5
Monday
Harbour Rocks

Blue Mountains — hire a car for the day

The Grand Canyon Track is a stunning 6.3 km rainforest loop (~3–4 hrs) — but it's at Blackheath, not Katoomba, and stitching it to the Three Sisters by train alone is a headache. A one-day car rental (or a small-group tour) unlocks the whole plateau.

  • Early start · Grand Canyon Track loop from Evans Lookout in the morning
  • Lunch in Blackheath or Leura
  • Three Sisters at Echo Point, and a section of the Prince Henry Cliff Walk
Check before you go: Blue Mountains tracks can close for weather, fire danger or maintenance. Look at the official NSW alerts the week prior.

02

Port Douglas & the Reef

The likely highlight. Fly into Cairns, drive the pretty hour north to Port Douglas, and settle in for five nights — close to the best outer reef and the oldest rainforest on earth. One base, no repacking.

Tropical north · 5 nights · reef + rainforest
Oct 6
Tuesday
Oaks Resort

Fly to Cairns, drive to Port Douglas

Pick up the car at Cairns airport and take the coast road north — one of the prettiest hours of driving in the country, rainforest on one side, Coral Sea on the other.

  • Quick stop at Palm Cove if the timing's right
  • Settle in; sunset and dinner on Macrossan Street
Oct 7
Wednesday
Oaks Resort

Outer Great Barrier Reef with Wavelength Booked

Locked in for 2 — a snorkel-focused, small-group day on the outer reef, on your first full day so the later days stay free as weather backups.

Check-in 8:10 am — it's an early, full day on the water. Keep tonight's dinner low-key and close by (IronBar or Sassi both fit); you'll be sun-and-salt tired.

Oct 8
Thursday
Oaks Resort

Daintree & Cape Tribulation

Give the rainforest the whole day — it's the oldest on the planet, and the one place where reef meets jungle at the shoreline.

  • Mossman Gorge · the Daintree ferry · rainforest boardwalks · Cape Tribulation
Start early and skip a late-evening plan afterward — tropical roads after dark aren't fun, and wildlife wanders onto them.
Oct 9
Friday
Oaks Resort

Atherton Tablelands — waterfall country

The one long-driving day inland (~4+ hrs total behind the wheel), but the payoff is exactly your kind of thing: a string of waterfalls and freshwater swimming holes, cool and green.

  • Curtain Fig Tree · Yungaburra · Lake Eacham · Millaa Millaa Falls · a waterfall stop or two more
Worth the drive if swimming under waterfalls sounds good. If you'd rather bank a slow day, this is the one to trade for a beach day instead.
Oct 10
Saturday
Oaks Resort

Flex day — reef again, or do nothing at all

Keep this open on purpose. Use it as your weather backup for the reef, or a second, gentler water day, or simply Four Mile Beach and the pool.

  • Low Isles sailing & snorkel (calmer, island setting) · or reef weather-backup · or a proper resort day
Stinger season note: October edges toward the warmer marine-stinger months (roughly Nov–May). Follow your operator's advice and wear the protective suit when they say so.

03

Melbourne & the Great Ocean Road

Fly Cairns to Melbourne direct, spend real time in the city, then take the one southern drive that earns every kilometre — two nights down the coast, two different towns, no backtracking.

Southern coast · 5 nights · the good drive
Oct 11
Sunday
Vibe Hotel

Fly Cairns → Melbourne

A direct hop (~3.5 hrs) swaps tropics for temperate in an afternoon. Check into the Vibe on Queen St (from 3 pm), no car needed yet — Melbourne is a walking-and-tram town.

Oct 12
Monday
Vibe Hotel

A full Melbourne day

Give the city its due — it rewards wandering more than ticking sights. Coffee is a competitive sport here; lean in.

  • CBD laneways & arcades · Fitzroy or Collingwood · Carlton & Lygon Street · Southbank
  • Long dinner, good wine, better coffee
Queen Victoria Market is closed Mondays — so save it for tomorrow morning on your way to the car.
Oct 13
Tuesday
Sandpiper Motel

Market, then drive to Apollo Bay

Grab the car after a Queen Vic Market breakfast and start the coast road. Today is the surf-coast stretch — take it in stages.

  • Torquay · Bells Beach · the Memorial Arch · Lorne & Teddy's Lookout · Kennett River koalas · Apollo Bay for the night
Sleep in Apollo Bay, not Lorne — it sets you up far better for the Otways and the Apostles tomorrow.
Oct 14
Wednesday
Loch Ard Inn

Otways rainforest & the Shipwreck Coast

The scenic climax. Rainforest in the morning, the big sea stacks in the golden afternoon light, then sleep close enough to catch them again at dawn.

  • Maits Rest rainforest walk · Cape Otway · Gibson Steps · Twelve Apostles · Loch Ard Gorge · The Razorback · Port Campbell
Staying in Port Campbell means the Apostles at sunset and sunrise, with no rush back to the city in between.
Oct 15
Thursday
Mantra Airport

Last coastal sights, inland home

Catch the final viewpoints in morning light, then cut inland via Colac — a faster route back than re-driving the whole coast — and return the car, then check in at the Mantra by the airport for an effortless morning.

  • Twelve Apostles at sunrise (if you're up for it) · London Bridge · The Grotto · Bay of Islands
  • Inland to Melbourne · drop the rental at the airport depot · dinner near the hotel
Returning the car this evening (not tomorrow morning) means zero stress before the flight. Confirm your depot's closing hours when you book the rental.
Oct 16
Friday
Fly home

Home

You're minutes from the terminal at the Mantra, with a free airport shuttle — so the morning's easy. Aim to be at the terminal by ~8:30 am for the 11:00 am departure.
